**Latin name:** Strongyloides papillosus

**Affects:** Small stock and cattle

**Primary Organ System:** Gastrointestinal

**Secondary Organ System:** Respiratory

**Identification tips:** Small-sized parthenogenic females only (6 mm); colourless; oval medium-sized thin-shelled larvated egg; lymphatic-tracheal migration; somatic migration in immune hosts; PPP 3–14 days

**Clinical Signs:**

- Intermittent watery diarrhoea

- Reduced growth rate and weight loss

- Inappetence

**Pathological signs:**

- Skin/lungs: Migratory phase lesions

- Intestine: Catarrhal enteritis, villous atrophy, malabsorption, hypoproteinaemia, oedema

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Percutaneous/transmammary/ingestion infection → lymphatic-tracheal migration → catarrhal enteritis → villous atrophy → malabsorption → hypoproteinaemia → oedema

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Demonstration of eggs in fresh faecal samples; faecal flotation; faecal cultures with identification of larvae

- Demonstration of intestinal stages in mucosal scrapings at PM

**Zoonosis:** yes — humans act as paratenic hosts. Percutaneous infection from damp contaminated ground (cutaneous larva migrans)
