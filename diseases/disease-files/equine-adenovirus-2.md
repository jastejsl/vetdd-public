**Specific Causes:** Equine mastadenovirus B (EAdV-2)

**Affects:** Horses

**Primary Organ System:** Digestive (GIT)

**Clinical Signs:**

- Typically subclinical

- GI infections in foals

**Pathological signs:**

- GIT: intestinal epithelial involvement

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation; antibody tests
