**Specific Causes:** Bovine alphaherpesvirus 1 / BoHV-1

Subtype 1 (BoHV-1.1): respiratory tract disease, conjunctivitis, abortion

Subtype 2 (BoHV-1.2): venereal disease (IPV/IPB)

**Affects:** Cattle

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory; Reproductive

**Secondary Organ System:** Ocular; Hepatic (foetal)

**Transmission:** Coitus or fomites (genital); aerosol (respiratory); semen; AI (artificial insemination)

**Clinical Signs:**

- Subtype 1 (respiratory/IBR): fever; coughing; mucopurulent nasal discharge; drop in milk production; erosions on nasal mucosa; secondary infections (Mannheimia haemolytica); abortions (foetuses autolysed/mummified)

- Subtype 2 (genital/IPV): cows — vesicles, pustules, ulcers in vulvovaginal mucosa; frequent urination; elevated tail; vaginal discharge; abortions

- Subtype 2 (genital/IPB): bulls — swollen prepuce; discharge; lesions on penis

- Infertility

**Pathological signs:**

- Reproductive: necrotizing endometritis; multifocal necrotising oophoritis; aborted foetuses mummified

- Respiratory: necrosis and erosions in respiratory epithelium

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Local lesions in mucous membranes: vesicles → pustules → erosions → ulcers → necrosis; latency in neurons (reactivation after stress); abortion: virus bypasses placental barrier → infects foetus via umbilical vein

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus culture: foetal lung, kidneys, spleen, liver, adrenal gland, placental tissue

- Antigen detection: IFA or ELISA (gE ELISA to differentiate vaccinated vs unvaccinated)

- PCR; serology

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes

**Related symptom**:

- Abortion storms in heifers (BoHV-1.1); pustular vulvovaginitis/balanoposthitis (BoHV-1.2)
