**Specific Causes:** Morbillivirus canis

**Affects****:** Dogs, Carnivora family

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory; Nervous

**Secondary Organ System:** Digestive; Integumentary; Urogenital; Lymphoid

**Transmission:** Aerosol; shed in aerosol droplets, urine and faeces; transplacental spread possible

**Clinical Signs:**

- Biphasic fever

- Lymphopenia / absolute lymphopaenia

- Oculo-nasal discharge (mucopurulent)

- Diarrhoea, vomiting, anorexia

- Cough; respiratory distress

- Pustular dermatitis

- Enamel hypoplasia (young dogs)

Acute:

- Mild: listlessness, decreased appetite, fever, mild upper respiratory tract infection

- Severe multisystemic: mucopurulent eye and nose secretions, anorexia, listlessness, high temperature, cough, vomiting, diarrhoea

- Neurological (grey matter): seizures, myoclonus with hyperaesthesia and depression; ataxia, paresis, paralysis, tremors

- Old dog encephalitis: loss of mentality, paresis, blindness, convulsions, myoclonic seizures

**Pathological signs:**

- Respiratory: conjunctivitis, rhinitis, pharyngitis; pulmonary oedema; interstitial pneumonia

- Lymphoid: lymph node congestion; splenomegaly; thymic involution

- Integumentary: pustular dermatitis

- GIT: secondary infections due to immune suppression

- CNS: neuronal damage (grey matter); demyelination (white matter)

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Virus enters respiratory tract → spreads via macrophages through lymphatics to spleen, intestines, mesenteric lnn. and liver → further spread to epithelial cells and CNS due to immunosuppression → acute encephalitis (grey matter neuronal damage) or chronic encephalitis (white matter demyelination)

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Haematology: anaemia, distemper inclusions in lymphocytes/neutrophils/erythrocytes

- Direct immunofluorescence for antigen detection (conjunctival and tonsillar smears)

- Serology: ELISA, indirect IF for antibodies

- PCR

- Histopathology (whole brain, spinal cord, lungs, kidneys in formalin)

**Lab Findings**:

- Haematology: absolute lymphopaenia; distemper inclusions in lymphocytes, neutrophils and erythrocytes

- Direct immunofluorescence: apple-green positive result

- Histopathology: brown colouring on tissues
