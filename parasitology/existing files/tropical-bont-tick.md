**Primary Organ System:** Cardiovascular / CNS

**Secondary Organ System:** Integument

**Identification tips:** 3-host ornate bont tick; tropical distribution; colourful scutal markings; vectors multiple pathogens

**Carrier of:** Ehrlichia ruminantium (Heartwater); Dermatophilus congolensis

**Clinical Signs:**

Clinical signs from transmitted agents

- Heartwater: fever, neurological signs, hydropericardium

- Dermatophilus congolensis: dermatophilosis (rain rot)

**Pathological signs:**

- Cardiovascular: hydropericardium (Heartwater)

- Integument: crusty skin lesions (Dermatophilus congolensis)

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Tick feeds → inoculates E. ruminantium → endothelial cell infection → vasculitis → hydropericardium and neurological signs;

- Similar pathophysiology for Dermatophilus congolensis

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Identification of tropical bont tick
