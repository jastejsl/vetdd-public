**Common names:** 

- Bovine respiratory disease complex (BRDC)

- Thromboembolic meningoencephalitis (TEME)

- Sleeper syndrome

**Affects:** Cattle (beef feedlot cattle primarily)

**Primary Organ System:** Nervous system

**Secondary Organ System:** Cardiovascular; Respiratory

**Clinical Signs:**

- Fever, depression

- Lateral recumbency and closed eyes (sleeper syndrome)

- Ataxia, weakness, may appear blind

- Neurological disease: acutely affected and sudden death

- Myocarditis: very rapid; sudden death may be the only sign

- Respiratory form: non-specific signs consistent with any pneumonic calf

**Pathological signs:**

- Heart: Left ventricular free wall most commonly affected; full-thickness myocardial pallor

- Brain: Brain infarcts (vasculitis, neutrophilic inflammation, necrosis, colonies of Gram-negative bacteria in thrombi)

- Microscopic: Vasculitis, neutrophilic inflammation, necrosis

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Commensal of URT → mucosal trauma → opportunistic infection established → vasculitis, thromboembolic lesions in brain and heart → sudden death/recumbency (neurological disease) OR myocarditis (cardiac disease) OR bronchopneumonia (respiratory disease)

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Gross and microscopic lesions (myocarditis, vasculitis, brain infarcts)

- Bacterial culture and identification
