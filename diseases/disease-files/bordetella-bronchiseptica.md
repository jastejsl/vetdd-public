**Affects:** Pigs, dogs, cats, rabbits, horses

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory 

**Transmission:** Direct contact; aerosol/droplet transmission; infected animals shed bacteria in nasal secretions

**Clinical Signs:**

- **Pigs (bronchopneumonia — piglets 3–4 days old):** Fever; anorexia; coughing and dyspnoea; secondary infections can exacerbate pneumonic lesions

- **Pigs (Non-Progressive Atrophic Rhinitis — 13–17 weeks old):** Sneezing and snuffling; tear staining of hair and skin below the medial canthus of the eye; secondary infections can cause mucopurulent rhinitis

- **Pigs (Progressive Atrophic Rhinitis — 13–17 weeks old):** Like non-progressive but more severe; epistaxis; severe snout deformities; growth retardation

- **Dogs:** Severe dry coughing and gagging; partial anorexia; incubation period 3–10 days; usually self-limiting; some infections progress to fatal bronchopneumonia

**Pathological signs:**

- Nasal cavity: Severe atrophy of turbinate scrolls; deformities of the nasal septum (non-progressive form); snout and facial bone involvement (progressive form)

- Lungs: Pneumonic lesions; secondary infections worsen consolidation

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- *B. bronchiseptica* colonizes ciliated respiratory epithelium of the nasal cavity → produces dermonecrotic toxin → mucosal damage allows *Pasteurella multocida* (type A or D) to co-colonize and multiply → *P. multocida* toxin and dermonecrotic toxin synergistically stimulate osteoclast proliferation → resorption of bone in nasal septum and turbinates → depression of osteoblast differentiation → non-progressive form causes turbinate scroll atrophy and nasal septal deformities; co-infection with *P. multocida* leads to progressive atrophic rhinitis involving snout and facial bones

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Bacterial culture and isolation from nasal swabs

- Histopathology of nasal turbinates

**Related Diseases**:

- Kennel Cough complex (dogs) — *Bordetella bronchiseptica* + canine parainfluenza virus + canine adenovirus type 2

- Porcine Atrophic Rhinitis complex — *Bordetella bronchiseptica* + *Pasteurella multocida* type A or D
