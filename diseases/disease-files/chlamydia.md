**Caused by:** Chlamydophila abortus

**Common names:** Enzootic abortion of ewes (EAE); Ovine enzootic abortion (OEA)

**Affects:** Sheep (primary), goats, cattle, humans (zoonotic)

**Primary Organ System:** Reproductive

**Secondary Organ System:** Lymphoid (latent infection in lymphoid tissues)

**Transmission:** Faecal-oral route (primary); aerosol; close contact; arthropods; large numbers shed during abortion/parturition via placenta, uterine discharges, faeces

**Clinical Signs:**

- Ewes: Acute necrotic placentitis

- Cotyledons thickened, yellow-pink with foci of necrosis at margins

- Intercotyledonary chorion tough, granular with yellow flaky material

- Purulent uterine discharges

- Late-term abortions (\~day 90 gestation)

- Aborted/weak lambs: congested and swollen livers with pinpoint white foci; lymph nodes enlarged and swollen; petechiae of skin and thymus

- Potential role in male infertility

**Pathological signs:**

- Placenta: Dark red cotyledons; thickened red intercotyledonary membranes; cream/yellow exudate adherent to placental surface; necrotic cotyledons

- Fetal liver: Congested and swollen with pinpoint white foci

- Fetal lymph nodes: Enlarged and swollen

- Fetal skin/thymus: Petechiae (septicaemia process)

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Oral/oro-pharyngeal infection (tonsils) → disseminated via blood (chlamydaemia) → latent infection in lymphoid tissues of non-pregnant animals (persistent bodies) → immune modulation in next pregnancy releases organism from persistent state → secondary chlamydaemia → placental infection via haematomas (day 60–90) → localization in chorionic epithelium of placentome → damage to chorionic epithelial cells → embryonic death/resorption/mummification/abortion (\~day 90)

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Modified acid-fast stain (Stamp's stain / Modified Ziehl-Neelsen) of impression smears

- Culture (Special transport medium SPG)

- Histopathology and immunohistochemistry

- PCR

- Serology: CFT or ELISA (acute at abortion + convalescent sera required; false positives due to Chlamydophila pecorum)

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes

**Zoonosis:** yes

**Human component**:

- In pregnant women: abortion, stillbirth, maternal illness; immunocompromised individuals: respiratory disease
