**Caused by:** Actinobacillus pleuropneumoniae

**Affects:** Pigs (all ages, mostly 12–15 weeks)

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory

**Secondary Organ System:** Cardiovascular

**Transmission:** Highly contagious respiratory spread; direct contact between pigs

**Clinical Signs:**

- Pyrexia (41°C), dyspnoea, mouth-breathing

- Sitting position, cyanotic skin

- Coughing, loss of appetite and growth retardation (chronic)

Acute:

- High fever, severe dyspnoea, mouth-breathing, sitting position, cyanotic skin

**Pathological signs:**

- Trachea: Haemorrhagic froth in windpipe

- Lungs: Generalized consolidation and firmness; blood-tinged fluid in chest and abdomen; abscesses throughout lung; lesions in dorsal lung and diaphragmatic lobe; whitish adhesions on lung surface and pleura; "strawberry" lesions on lungs

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Inhalation → colonization of respiratory tract → highly contagious respiratory disease → severe fibrinonecrotic pneumonia → pleuritis → respiratory failure and death

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Bacterial culture and isolation of causative organism

- Immunoperoxidase stains of formalin-fixed tissues

- ELISA, Complement Fixation, Indirect Hemagglutination test
