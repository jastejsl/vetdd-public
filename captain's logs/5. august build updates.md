# August build updates

August was spent making a lot of progress outside the scope of the main application; summated below.

### Literature enhancement

As AI tooling becomes an unavoidable component of our lives, especially in academia, it occurred to me that I can integrate AI tooling into the literature components such that everyone accessing academic articles via my app can do so & use AI in a more empowering manner.

Now, there exists the ability for a user to receive a high quality summary/analysis of any single academic article by clicking "Tools" → "Summarise" or "Analyse".

If a user also saves (in app) multiple articles, they can now create a high quality comparison between the articles by navigating to the "Saved" tab (in the header) → "Chat with articles" → "Compare" (after selecting the articles of interest).

"Dissertation" in the same "Chat with articles" block remains an experimental tooling option I've configured. Playing with it explains it in essence.

## Presentation of my app

This was possibly the biggest highlight of August.

### Feedback system revamp

This is possibly the second most important change that happened during the tail end of August.

It was always a vision of mine that the information in the app is not something I can consider as "my property" but rather the collective property of all those that use the app and contribute to it.

However, until the change described below, there was no way for the user to download or access the knowledge hosted in the app outside the bounds of the application itself. This has now changed.

Going forward, a user can now go to the feedback path, find the specific page for the card they wish to acquire the information for and copy it or download it as a PDF.&#x20;
