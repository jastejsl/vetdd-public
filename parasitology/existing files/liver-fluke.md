**Caused by:** Fasciola hepatica

**Affects:** 

- DH = ruminants (+ humans)

- IH = Lymnaea/Galba (aquatic/amphibious freshwater snails)

**Primary Organ System:** Hepatic

**Secondary Organ System:** Haematological

**Predilection site:** Bile ducts of liver

**Identification tips:** 

- Leaf-shaped

- Dorsoventrally flattened

- Brown/grey0

- Conical anterior end

- Oval large egg with fine granular material

- Yellow-brow

Metacercaria = circular, small, attached to herbage in water; 1 miracidium can produce \>600 metacercariae;

**Transmission:**

DH ingests metacercariae → excyst → penetrate intestinal wall → migrate in peritoneal cavity → liver parenchyma

**Clinical Signs:**

- Acute (sheep): Sudden death

- Subacute (sheep + calves): High mortality; anaemia + bottle jaw

- Chronic (sheep/cattle/horse/man — most common):
  - Sheep: anaemia, bottle jaw, weight loss
  - Cattle: production losses (milk)

**Pathological signs:**

- Liver: Traumatic hepatitis (immature flukes migrate in parenchyma); hepatic fibrosis; hyperplastic cholangitis ('pipe-stem liver' from calcification in cattle)

- Blood: Anaemia and hypoalbuminaemia from haematophagic adults → oedema (bottle jaw)

**Mechanism:**

- Ingestion of metacercariae → excyst → penetrate intestinal wall → juvenile flukes migrate in peritoneal cavity and liver parenchyma (traumatic hepatitis) → adults in bile ducts → haematophagic → anaemia/hypoalbuminaemia/oedema

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Faecal sedimentation (Benedek technique) — demonstrate eggs

**Zoonosis:** yes — humans can be DH
