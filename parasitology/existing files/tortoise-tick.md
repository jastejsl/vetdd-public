**Affects:** Tortoises; reptiles

**Primary Organ System:** Integument

**Predilection site:** Soft parts of tortoises around base of tail and legs

**Identification tips:** 3-host ornate ixodid tick; life cycle \>1 year; predominantly on tortoises and reptiles; attaches to soft skin around base of tail and legs

**Clinical Signs:**

- Local irritation and tissue damage at attachment sites

- Debilitation in heavy infestations

**Pathological signs:**

- Integument: attachment wounds on soft parts around base of tail and legs

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Tick attachment to soft skin → local tissue damage and blood loss

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Identification of tortoise tick on reptiles
