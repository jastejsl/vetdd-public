**Specific Causes:** Respirovirus bovis (previously Bovine parainfluenza virus 3)

**Affects:** Cattle; sheep; goats

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory

**Transmission:** Nasal and ocular secretions; aerosol transmission; contact with mucous membranes

**Clinical Signs:**

- Mild or clinically inapparent infection

- Common secondary infection: pneumonia (shipping fever)

**Pathological signs:**

- Respiratory: necrosis of ciliated epithelium of trachea, bronchi and alveoli; interferes with mucociliary clearance

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Virus infects epithelial cells in the alveoli → necrosis of ciliated epithelium → impaired mucociliary clearance → predisposes lung to bacterial invasion (shipping fever)

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation

- Detection of virus antigen

- Demonstration of rising antibody levels
